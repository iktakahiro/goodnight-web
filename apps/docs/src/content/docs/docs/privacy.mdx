## What is stored

A contact submission stores what the visitor typed (name, email address, company, phone number, category, and message), the time they agreed to the privacy policy, the page they sent it from, the user agent, the country that Cloudflare reports, and an `ip_hash`. The raw IP address is not stored. See [No raw IP addresses](/docs/how-it-works/ip-hashing/).

Inquiries are deleted after the retention period, 180 days by default. See [Deleting old inquiries without Cron](/docs/how-it-works/data-pruning/).

## Data is stored outside Japan

D1 lets you restrict where data is stored only to the EU, FedRAMP, or the US. You cannot pin a database to Japan ([Cloudflare: D1 data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/configuration/data-location/)). The privacy policy template therefore says that data may be stored on servers in other countries (such as the United States), names the storage provider, and says that the company has checked the privacy rules of those countries. Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information asks a business that handles personal data abroad to understand that environment. A comment in the template points to the country surveys published by the Personal Information Protection Commission, for when you describe those rules in detail.

## Deleted data can be restored for a while

D1 Time Travel can restore a database to an earlier point in time: up to 7 days back on the Workers free plan and up to 30 days on the paid plan ([Cloudflare: Time Travel](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/time-travel/)). A deleted inquiry is therefore fully gone only after that period. The template says that deletion takes effect fully after up to 7 days on the free plan; change it to 30 if you use the paid plan.

## Third parties

- **Turnstile.** The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. The template mentions it.
- **Slack.** If you turn on Slack notifications, the message contains only the count, the category, and a link. No personal data is sent to Slack.
- **Fonts.** No external fonts are loaded. The starter site, the admin screen, and this documentation site use the fonts already installed on the visitor's device (a system font stack), so no font service receives a request when someone opens a page.

## The template

The starter has the template in Japanese and English: `src/content/pages/ja/privacy.md` and `src/content/pages/en/privacy.md`. It uses the sample company's details, including the representative's name and the contact desk for requests to disclose, correct, or delete personal information. Replace them with your own, and remove the note at the top of the page after a professional has reviewed it.
