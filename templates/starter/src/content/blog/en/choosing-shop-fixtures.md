---
title: Five things to decide before ordering shop fixtures
description: Decisions that help you avoid mistakes when you order display shelves or counters.
date: 2026-08-28
tags: [Shops, Design]
---

Display shelves and counters are hard to change once they are built. If you decide these five things before you contact us, the planning goes faster.

1. **What you will display, and how many.** The size and number of items set the depth of the shelves and the number of levels.
2. **Aisle width.** For two people to pass each other, 90 cm or more is comfortable.
3. **Cleaning.** Furniture that reaches the floor collects less dust underneath.
4. **Power outlets.** Wiring for the register and lights affects the shape of the furniture.
5. **Future changes.** If you rearrange the shop each season, keep pieces small enough to move.

It is fine if you have not decided everything. We can work out the open points together when we measure.
