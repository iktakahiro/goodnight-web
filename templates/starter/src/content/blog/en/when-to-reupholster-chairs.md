Dozens of people sit on a shop's chairs every day. Seat fabric and cushions wear out sooner than most people expect.

## Signs it is time

Consider reupholstering when any of these is true:

- The fabric is worn through and the cushion shows
- You can feel the board under the seat when you sit down
- Stains do not come out, or a smell remains after cleaning

If you keep using a flattened cushion, the frame takes more stress and can start to wobble.

## Keeping the cost down

Reupholstering several chairs of the same model at once wastes less fabric and lowers the cost per chair. If you cannot send all of them at once, you can split them into two batches. We can also pick them up on your closing day and return them before you open again.
