With some care, a solid wood table lasts for decades. You do not need special tools. This article covers the basics for a table that is used every day in a shop.

## Every day

- Wipe the table with a well-wrung cloth, then dry it with a dry cloth.
- Disinfectant sprays with alcohol can damage the finish. If you use one, spray it on the cloth, not on the table.
- Hot dishes leave white rings. Use trivets or coasters.

## Once a year: oiling

An oil-finished table needs new oil when the surface starts to feel dry. Once a year is a good guide.

1. Clean the surface and let it dry well.
2. Sand it lightly along the grain with 240-grit sandpaper.
3. Put a little oil on a cloth and spread it thinly.
4. Wait about 15 minutes, then wipe off the extra oil with a dry cloth.
5. Let it dry overnight before using the table.

Cloths soaked with oil can catch fire on their own if they are left in a pile. Soak used cloths in water before you throw them away.
